Glitch anatomy

Idle glitch · five layers

The idle glitch is a short burst of stepped frames, 7 to 13 of them over roughly 250–450ms, that rise and fall in strength and then snap back to a clean frame. Each frame mixes up to five layers. Toggle layers and intensity below, then trigger it, or leave Auto on to see it at its real 8–20 second rhythm.

Graphs in motion

Live data · four rules

Live charts move because the data moves, never for decoration. Time scrolls at a constant rate, scales settle, new values snap in, and crossing a threshold is the one event that gets a flicker.
